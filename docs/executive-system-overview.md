## What This System Is

Royal Energy Alchemy runs on a custom-built practitioner operating system — a private, PIN-protected digital platform that handles client management, session tracking, outcome research, and AI-powered pattern intelligence, all in one place.

You own the entire system. No monthly SaaS fees for the core platform. No data shared with third parties. Every session record, every client note, every outcome is stored in your own private database.

---

## The Two Centers

### Practitioner Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
Your daily operations hub. Use this before, during, and after every session.

- Book and log sessions
- Record outcomes, energy shifts, and notable findings
- Flag sessions for research
- Send aftercare follow-ups
- Manage client goals and product recommendations

### Research Intelligence Center (`/research.html`)
Your analytics and pattern workspace. Use this weekly or when preparing content, case studies, or service evaluations.

- See aggregate outcome statistics across all clients
- Review AI-detected patterns in your session data
- Browse published case studies and research insights
- Analyze which products and services perform best
- Surface research flags worth deeper investigation

---

## What the System Tracks

| Data Type | What It Captures |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Clients** | Name, contact info, session history |
| **Sessions** | Date, service type, duration, practitioner notes |
| **Outcomes** | Improvement category, level (1–10), energy shift, research notes |
| **Goals** | Client goals, target dates, achievement status |
| **Aftercare** | Follow-up messages, satisfaction scores, return intent |
| **Recommendations** | Products recommended, whether client found them helpful |
| **Patterns** | AI-detected statistical patterns across session and outcome data |
| **Insights** | Curated research insights ready for content or publication |
| **Case Studies** | Anonymized session narratives with problem/intervention/outcome |

## AI Capabilities

The system includes a built-in AI pattern detection engine. When you click **Run Detection** in the Research Intelligence Center, it automatically scans your session and outcome data to surface:

- Recurring client concerns across sessions
- Intervention patterns that correlate with improvement
- Service-level performance trends
- Recommendation effectiveness patterns

These patterns feed the Research Insights library and can become the foundation for content, case studies, or practitioner methodology documentation.

---

## Access & Security

The system is protected by a practitioner PIN. Only people with the PIN can access the dashboard or research center. The PIN is verified server-side — it cannot be bypassed in the browser.

Data is stored in a private Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. No client data is publicly accessible.
