# Practitioner User Guide

**Royal Energy Alchemy — Daily Workflow Reference**

---

## Getting In

Go to **https://royal-energy-alchemy.netlify.app/dashboard** (or `/research.html` for the Research Center). Enter your 6-digit PIN when prompted.

Your session stays active in the browser tab. If you open a new tab or browser, you'll be asked for your PIN again.

---

## Daily Workflow

### Before a Session
1. Open the **Clients** section → find or create the client
2. Review their session history and active goals
3. Note any outstanding aftercare from previous sessions

### During / After a Session
1. Go to **Sessions** → log the session (service type, date, notes)
2. If there was a notable outcome, open **Outcomes** → add an outcome record
   - Choose category: `improved`, `mixed`, `no_change`, or `worse`
   - Set improvement level 1–10 if applicable
   - Add energy shift description and notable findings
   - Toggle **Research Flag** if this session is worth deeper study
3. Add any product or supplement recommendations under **Recommendations**
4. Create or update a **Goal** record if the client has a specific healing intention

### After a Session (Follow-up)
1. Open **Aftercare** → draft a follow-up message or care instructions
2. When the client responds, update their satisfaction score and perceived improvement

## Key Sections

### Clients
- Full client list with session counts
- Click any client to see their full history
- Use the search/filter to find clients quickly

### Sessions
- Log every session here — this is the foundation of all your research data
- Service types: Distance Healing, In-Person, Chakra Alignment, Quantum Healing, Crystal Therapy, Sound Healing, Aura Cleansing

### Outcomes
- Outcome records are separate from session notes — they're structured for research
- The improvement level (1–10) feeds the AI pattern detection engine
- Research flags surface in the Research Intelligence Center

### Recommendations
- Track every product or supplement you recommend
- After the client tries it, update the outcome status: `helpful`, `purchased`, `declined`, or `recommended`
- This data powers the Recommendation Intelligence report

### Goals
- Client goals with categories: healing, energy, mental, physical, spiritual, emotional, general
- Set a target date and expected outcome
- Update status to `achieved` when the client reaches their goal — this feeds retention analytics

## Tips

**Flag generously.** When in doubt, flag a session for research. You can always un-flag it later. Flagged sessions are the raw material for case studies and insights.

**Log outcomes every time.** Even `no_change` outcomes are data. The more outcomes you log, the more meaningful the AI pattern detection becomes.

**Run Detection weekly.** The pattern engine scans all your data each time. Running it regularly means patterns surface as your dataset grows.

**Use goals for long-term clients.** Clients with active goals show up in retention analytics. Marking a goal `achieved` is one of the most meaningful data points in the system.

## Troubleshooting

**Page won't load / PIN rejected:** Try a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R or Cmd+Shift+R). If PIN is rejected, contact your system administrator.

**API error on a section:** Use the **↺ Refresh** button in the top bar. If it persists, the issue is likely a temporary network problem.

**Data I entered isn't showing:** Refresh the section using the ↺ button. The system fetches live data from the database each time.
