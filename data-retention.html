Data Retention Policy

Royal Energy Alchemy LLC

Effective Date: June 2026 · Erie / Waterford, Pennsylvania

This Data Retention Policy explains how long Royal Energy Alchemy LLC retains different types of client and business information, and how clients may request deletion of their personal data.

Retention Schedule

Record Type Retention Period Reason
Client intake forms and consent documents 7 years after last service Business records, legal compliance, dispute resolution
Session notes and treatment plans 7 years after last service Service continuity, documentation, client support
Session recordings Up to 3 years after session, unless extended by written consent Documentation, AI-assisted notes, quality assurance
Post-session check-in responses 7 years after last service Treatment tracking, longitudinal outcome records
Testimonials and ratings (approved) Until withdrawal of consent or business closure Published with client permission; removed on request
Payment records and transaction history 7 years Tax compliance, financial records (IRS standard)
Communications (email, messages) 3 years after last contact Business records, dispute resolution
Website technical data (IP, browser logs) Up to 12 months Security monitoring, analytics
De-identified aggregate data Indefinitely Research, reporting, service development; no personal identifiers retained
Certain records may be retained longer than the periods above if required by applicable federal or Pennsylvania state law, if the records are subject to an ongoing legal matter, or if the client has provided separate consent for extended retention.

Deletion Requests

Clients may request deletion of their personal information at any time by contacting us at royalenergyalchemy@gmail.com. Deletion requests are subject to applicable legal obligations — we may be required to retain certain records regardless of a deletion request (for example, payment records for tax compliance).

When a deletion request is received, we will:

1. Confirm receipt within 7 business days
2. Review which records are subject to deletion versus legal retention requirements
3. Complete eligible deletions within 30 days
4. Notify you of what was deleted and what was retained with explanation

Security During Retention

Records retained under this policy are maintained with reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards including password-protected systems, limited access controls, and encrypted connections. Physical records, if any, are stored securely and disposed of by shredding when the retention period expires.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. The effective date above will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
