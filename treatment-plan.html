05 Home Aftercare Protocol

What Daron clears in a session, your environment can reintroduce. The following four practices reinforce the work energetically — in your home, your body, and your field. Begin these the day of your session and continue for a minimum of two weeks.

🧂 Home Salt Cleansing

Use sea salt or Himalayan salt to physically and energetically clean your home. Sprinkle along doorways and windowsills. Mop floors with salt water. Replace every 3 days for the first week after your session.

🎵 Sound High Vibrational Audio

Play 432hz, 528hz, or Solfeggio frequencies in your home daily. These frequencies repel low vibrational entities and attachments. YouTube and Spotify have free playlists.

Play for a minimum of 30 minutes per day, especially in the room where you sleep.

🛁 Body Physical Protection Protocol
  • Salt or baking soda foot soak before bed — ½ cup in warm water, soak 15 minutes
  • Place salt under your bed in a small dish — refresh weekly
  • Repeat this affirmation at bedtime:

"My energy is my own. I am protected. Nothing that does not belong to me may remain."

Therapist & Medical Coordination

Energetic clearing moves things. What surfaces emotionally or physically after a session may need professional support to be fully processed. This is not a limitation of the work — it is how the work integrates.

Daron recommends scheduling a session with your therapist or primary care provider within one week of your 72-hour check-in. If you do not have a provider, ZocDoc can match you to a licensed therapist near you at little or no cost through your insurance.
