Questions
Do I need to complete each level before moving to the next? +
Foundations of Energy is strongly recommended before Applied Energy Work because the second level builds on the awareness, protection, and daily practices established in the first.
Will training be live, recorded, or both? +
Both. Daron will offer live sessions for real-time interaction and Q&A, with recordings available for those who cannot attend live. Details on format and scheduling will be shared with waitlist members first.
Is this training a certification program? +
This is a focused learning path for personal and practical energetic development. Any completion recognition will be announced closer to launch.
When does training launch? +
No launch date has been set yet. Waitlist members will be the first to know. Foundations of Energy will launch before Applied Energy Work.
Can I apply to be a provider even if I haven't taken Daron's training? +
Yes. The provider application is open to experienced practitioners regardless of whether they have completed Royal Energy Alchemy training. All applicants are reviewed for ethics, experience, approach, and alignment with Daron's values and standards.
Will there be pricing information available soon? +
Pricing will be announced to waitlist members before public release. Joining the waitlist ensures you see it first.
