Questions
Do I need to complete each level before moving to the next? +
Foundations is strongly recommended before Intermediate, and Intermediate before Advanced. Each level builds on the one before it. That said, if you already have relevant experience Daron may assess readiness individually.
Will training be live, recorded, or both? +
Both. Daron will offer live sessions for real-time interaction and Q&A, with recordings available for those who cannot attend live. Details on format and scheduling will be shared with waitlist members first.
Is this training a certification program? +
The Advanced level is designed with practitioner development in mind. Details on any completion recognition or certification structure will be announced closer to launch.
When does training launch? +
No launch date has been set yet. Waitlist members will be the first to know. Foundations will launch before Intermediate or Advanced.
Will there be pricing information available soon? +
Pricing will be announced to waitlist members before public release. Joining the waitlist ensures you see it first.
