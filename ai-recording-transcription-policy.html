AI Use, Recording & Transcription Disclosure

Royal Energy Alchemy LLC

Effective Date: June 2026 · Erie / Waterford, Pennsylvania · Version v1

This disclosure explains how Royal Energy Alchemy LLC may use session recording, transcription, and artificial-intelligence (AI) tools to support your care and our administrative work. It is a companion to our Privacy Policy and Recording Policy.

1. Recording of Sessions

Sessions are not recorded by default. Audio recording occurs only when it is explicitly requested and confirmed by you in advance. You may decline recording at any time, and declining will not affect the quality of your care. Pennsylvania is a two-party consent state (18 Pa. C.S. § 5703); any recording requires your documented consent.

2. Transcription

Where a session has been recorded with your consent, an automated transcription may be generated to assist with accurate notes. Transcription is used only when explicitly requested and confirmed, and the resulting text is handled as confidential client information under our Privacy Policy.

3. AI-Assisted Tools

We may use AI tools to assist with administrative tasks such as drafting session summaries, organizing notes, and preparing follow-up messages. AI assistance is a support tool only. AI-generated summaries are not final clinical, medical, legal, or psychological determinations, and a human practitioner reviews and remains responsible for any record or recommendation.

4. How Your Information Is Handled

Any recording, transcript, or AI-assisted output is treated as confidential client information and handled according to our Privacy Policy, including its data-retention and security provisions. We do not sell this information, and we do not share identifiable session content without your written consent.

5. Declining Recording or Transcription

You may decline recording or transcription before or during any session — simply tell your practitioner, or contact us at royalenergyalchemy@gmail.com or 814-392-2095. If you decline, written notes may still be kept to document your care.
