This Payment Policy explains how and when payment is due for services with Royal Energy Alchemy LLC. Please also review our Cancellation Policy.

1. Payment Due at Scheduling

Payment, or any required deposit, is due at the time of scheduling unless other arrangements have been confirmed in writing. Your appointment time is reserved once payment or deposit is received.

2. Deposits

Certain services or appointment times may require a non-refundable deposit to secure the booking. Where a deposit applies, it is credited toward the total service cost. The deposit amount is disclosed at the time of scheduling.

4. Refund Limits

Refunds are governed together with our Cancellation Policy. Timely cancellations may be refunded or credited; deposits may be non-refundable; and completed sessions are generally non-refundable. All refunds are subject to manual review.

5. No-Show Rules

Appointments missed without notice (no-shows) are non-refundable, and any prepaid amount or deposit may be retained to compensate for the reserved time. See the Cancellation Policy for details and emergency exceptions.

6. Unpaid Balances

Any balance remaining after a session is due promptly. Future appointments may not be scheduled while a balance is outstanding. We will contact you regarding any unpaid balance and work with you on reasonable arrangements where appropriate.

7. Packages & Payment Plans

Where multi-session packages or payment plans are offered, their specific terms — including price, number of sessions, expiration, and refundability — are provided in writing at purchase. Package sessions are typically non-transferable and may expire after a stated period.
