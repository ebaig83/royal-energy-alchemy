Recording Policy

Royal Energy Alchemy LLC

Effective Date: June 2026 · Erie / Waterford, Pennsylvania · Version v1

This Recording Policy explains when and how sessions may be recorded. It works together with our AI Use, Recording & Transcription Disclosure and Privacy Policy.

1. Consent Is Required

Pennsylvania is a two-party (all-party) consent state under 18 Pa. C.S. § 5703. We do not record any session without your explicit, documented consent obtained in advance. Recording never happens silently or by default.

2. When Recording May Occur

  • Only when you have explicitly requested or agreed to recording, and it has been confirmed before the session begins.
  • Recording is used to support accurate documentation, AI-assisted summaries, treatment planning, and your aftercare.
  • You will always know in advance if a session is to be recorded.

3. Declining or Withdrawing Consent

You may decline recording at any time, including during a session. You may also withdraw a prior recording consent for future sessions by telling your practitioner or contacting us. Declining will not reduce the quality or availability of your care.

4. Storage, Use & Retention

Recordings are treated as confidential client information and stored securely. They are used only for documentation, service delivery, and quality purposes, and are retained and deleted in accordance with our Data Retention Policy. Optional media releases (for educational or promotional use) are entirely voluntary and collected separately.

5. Access

Access to recordings is limited to the practitioner and authorized service providers bound by confidentiality. We do not share recordings with third parties without your written consent except as required by law.

Spiritual and energetic services are complementary wellness offerings and are not medical, legal, or psychological advice.
