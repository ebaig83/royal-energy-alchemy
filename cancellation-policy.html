Cancellation Policy

Royal Energy Alchemy LLC

Effective Date: June 2026 · Erie / Waterford, Pennsylvania · Version v1

We hold your appointment time specifically for you. This policy explains how cancellations, reschedules, and missed appointments are handled. Please also review our Payment Policy.

1. Cancellation Window

We ask that you cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your scheduled appointment. Cancellations made within this window allow us to offer the time to another client.

2. Rescheduling

  • You may reschedule at no charge when you give at least 24 hours' notice.
  • Reschedule requests can be made through your appointment-management link or by contacting us directly.
  • One same-day reschedule may be accommodated at our discretion depending on availability.

3. Refunds

Cancellations made with at least 24 hours' notice are eligible for a refund or full credit of any prepaid amount, subject to the limits in our Payment Policy. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may forfeit a deposit or be charged a late-cancellation fee.

4. No-Show / No-Call

If you do not attend a scheduled appointment and do not contact us, it is considered a no-show. No-shows are generally non-refundable, and any prepaid amount or deposit may be retained to compensate for the reserved time.

5. Emergency Exceptions

We understand that genuine emergencies and unexpected illness happen. If you experience an emergency that prevents you from attending, please contact us as soon as you are able. Emergency situations are reviewed compassionately and individually.

6. Manual Review

All fees, exceptions, and refund decisions under this policy are subject to manual review by Royal Energy Alchemy LLC. We aim to be fair and flexible, and we reserve reasonable discretion in applying this policy to individual circumstances.

Spiritual and energetic services are complementary wellness offerings and are not medical, legal, or psychological advice.
